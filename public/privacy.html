Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 14, 2025

1. Introduction

Repo Arborist ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • GitHub Token: Stored locally on your device to access GitHub API
  • GitHub Repository Data: Repository information you choose to visualize

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Crash Reports: Anonymous crash data via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version (for debugging purposes only)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app functionality
  • Visualize your GitHub repository data
  • Improve app stability through crash analytics
  • Enhance user experience

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: Your GitHub token is stored securely on your device using encrypted storage
  • Cloud Storage: We do not store your personal data on our servers
  • Firebase: Anonymous crash reports are stored on Firebase Crashlytics

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reporting and analytics
  • GitHub API: To fetch your repository data

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Your GitHub token and repository data remain on your device.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your locally stored data by uninstalling the app
  • Revoke GitHub token access at any time
  • Request information about data we collect

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
